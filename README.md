# Energy Data Analytics: Python Foundations

## Overview

This repository documents my journey towards applying Python, data analysis and quantitative methods to the energy sector.

The project combines my background in chemical engineering, sustainable energy research and low-carbon energy consultancy with practical programming and data analytics skills. Using real Great Britain (GB) electricity data, I am developing the ability to clean, analyse and visualise energy datasets and communicate evidence-based findings.

The repository currently focuses on Python fundamentals, data manipulation with Pandas, exploratory data analysis and visualisation, using electricity generation, demand and carbon intensity as the main application areas.

## Objectives

- Build a strong foundation in Python for data analysis.
- Develop practical skills in Pandas, data cleaning, aggregation and visualisation.
- Work with real-world electricity generation, demand and carbon intensity data.
- Investigate relationships between the GB electricity generation mix, electricity demand and carbon intensity.
- Develop reproducible analytical workflows and communicate findings clearly.
- Establish a foundation for future work in energy analytics, time-series analysis and data engineering.

## Projects and Learning Log

### Week 1 — Python Fundamentals for Energy Analysis

**Focus:** Core Python concepts and quantitative calculations.

Topics covered:
- Variables, data types, lists, loops and conditional statements.
- Functions and reusable calculations.
- Energy-related calculations and unit conversions.
- Power, energy and carbon emissions.

**Outcome:** Applied basic programming concepts to simple energy calculations, connecting Python fundamentals with practical energy-sector examples.

### Week 2 — Exploring GB Electricity Demand

**Focus:** Data manipulation and descriptive statistics using Pandas.

Topics covered:
- Loading and inspecting CSV datasets.
- Checking data types, missing values and duplicate records.
- Cleaning and preparing data.
- Grouping observations by month and calculating summary statistics.
- Investigating demand patterns and periods of high electricity demand.

**Outcome:** Used a real electricity demand dataset to explore how GB electricity demand varies over time and developed a repeatable workflow for inspecting and analysing tabular data.


### Week 3 — Exploring Electricity Carbon Intensity

**Focus:** Exploratory data analysis and visualisation of time-series data.

Topics covered:
- Working with timestamps and extracting temporal features.
- Analysing hourly and day-of-week patterns.
- Grouping and aggregating observations.
- Visualising distributions and temporal trends.
- Calculating rolling averages to identify broader patterns.

**Outcome:** Investigated how GB electricity carbon intensity varies throughout the day and week, using visualisations to identify patterns in the data.


### Week 4 — Investigating Factors Associated with Carbon Intensity

**Focus:** Combining energy datasets and investigating relationships between electricity generation, demand and carbon intensity.

Topics covered:
- Combining generation and demand datasets using timestamps.
- Creating derived variables and generation-share calculations.
- Comparing generation patterns during high- and low-carbon periods.
- Exploring relationships between gas, wind, solar, nuclear generation, demand and carbon intensity.
- Communicating findings through charts and summary statistics.

**Outcome:** Developed an exploratory analysis of the factors associated with higher and lower GB electricity carbon intensity, using generation mix and demand comparisons to investigate observed patterns.
